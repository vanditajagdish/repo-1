Chapter by chapter

Our Story

Some stories begin with fireworks. Ours began quietly, and grew into something that fills every room we walk into together.

The Meeting Chapter I

It started with a mutual friend, a crowded table and a conversation that refused to end. By the time the evening was over, we had argued about books, agreed on chai over coffee, and swapped numbers “just to send that playlist.”

The First Date Chapter II

A long walk along the Juhu seafront, cutting chai in paper cups and a sunset neither of us was really watching. We said goodbye three times before either of us actually left.

Growing Together Chapter III

New cities, late-night drives, family dinners that ran past midnight and two families slowly becoming one. Somewhere along the way, “you and me” quietly became “us.”

The Question Chapter IV

One ring, a bunch of white flowers and a very nervous Kunwar. Saloni said yes before the question was even finished — and the rest, as they say, is a very long guest list.

Forever 2nd December 2026

And now the next chapter — written at The Club, Juhu, in front of everyone we love. We can’t wait to celebrate it with you.
