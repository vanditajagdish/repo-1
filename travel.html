Getting here

Arrivals

I By air

Fly into Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM). The venue is roughly 8–10 km from the terminals — about 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.

II By rail

The nearest suburban stations are Andheri and Vile Parle on the Western Line, a short auto-rickshaw or cab ride from the club.

III Around town

Uber and Ola work well across Mumbai, and black-and-yellow taxis and autos are everywhere. We’ll also run shuttles between partner hotels and the venue for every event.

Good to know

Weather & packing

  • Early December is pleasant: around 22–32 °C, dry and sunny.
  • Evenings by the sea can be breezy — a light shawl is handy.
  • Comfortable shoes for the lawns (and the dance floor).
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen for the haldi.
While you’re here

Around Juhu

  • Sunset walk and street food on Juhu Beach.
  • Prithvi Theatre and its café, just around the corner.
  • Bandra’s Bandstand, Carter Road and Pali Hill cafés.
  • South Mumbai: Gateway of India, Colaba & Kala Ghoda.
